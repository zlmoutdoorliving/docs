Deals by Text: Privacy Policy

ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living · Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains how ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living ("ZLM," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses and protects information for our promotional text program, ZLM Outdoor Living Deals, sent from (810) 246-5330. See the Deals by Text Terms for how the program works.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information: your mobile number, and your name or email if you give them (for example on the sign-up page or with a store order).
  • Consent records: the date, time, method (text JOIN, sign-up page, store checkout or Client Hub), the wording you agreed to, and the IP address or order number it came from, plus every opt-out.
  • Message records: the Messages we send you, your replies, and delivery information from our messaging provider.

2. How we use it

  • To send you the deal, new-arrival and showroom-event Messages you signed up for, up to 4 per month.
  • To honor STOP, START and HELP requests and keep records of your consent and opt-outs.

3. How we share information

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver Messages, such as our messaging platform provider (Twilio).
  • We do not sell your information.

4. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep consent and opt-out records for as long as needed to show compliance. To ask us to delete your information, call (810) 213-8010 or email office@zlmoutdoorliving.com.

5. Security

We store this information with access limited to ZLM staff and the service providers that run our systems.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new effective date.
