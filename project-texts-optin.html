How customers opt in to project texts

ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living · November 16, 2026

Customers of ZLM Outdoor Living (an outdoor living design, construction and furnishings contractor in Casco, Michigan) choose to receive project texts by checking an optional, unchecked box. The box is never pre-checked and is never required to request a quote, book, sign a contract or use the Client Hub. We store the date, time, source, IP address and exact wording of each consent, and every opt-out (STOP, or turning texts off in the Client Hub). This page shows exactly what customers see.

1. Client Hub (clients.zlmoutdoorliving.com and the ZLM Client Hub app)

Clients get a private Client Hub page for their project. On its overview, until they decide, they see this card:

Get project texts?
Mobile number on file: (810) 555-0123. Not right? Call (810) 213-8010 and we'll fix it.
Save Not now

Clients who don't opt in are updated by email and in the Client Hub only. Clients who opt in can turn texts off from the same page at any time.

2. Website quote request (Ask ZLM chat on zlmoutdoorliving.com)

When a customer requests a quote in the chat on our website, the summary card they confirm includes this box above the "Send my request" button:

Your quote request
Name, phone, email, address, project, timeline and budget are shown here for the customer to confirm.

3. Online booking (booking.zlmoutdoorliving.com)

The booking form has a separate optional, unchecked box for appointment texts only, under our Appointment Text Message Terms.

4. Signed quote terms

Every ZLM quote includes our terms. Section 14, "Communication," reads: "By approving this quote, the Client agrees that ZLM Outdoor Living and its staff may contact the Client about this project and related service, warranty and billing matters by phone call, email and text message (SMS/MMS) at the phone numbers and email addresses the Client provided, including texts from the work phone lines of ZLM staff. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts from that number, or HELP for help. This consent is not required to purchase. We do not use these texts for marketing." Clients approve the quote by signature or online approval.

5. Customer texts us first

If a customer texts one of our numbers, staff reply within that conversation.

First message after opting in

"ZLM Outdoor Living: You're signed up for project texts — scheduling, on-my-way notices, project updates and messages from your project team. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
