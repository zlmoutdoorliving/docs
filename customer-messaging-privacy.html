Customer Messaging Privacy Policy

ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living · Effective November 11, 2026

ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living ("ZLM," "we," "us," or "our"), designs, builds and furnishes outdoor living spaces and runs a showroom in Casco, Michigan. This policy explains what information we receive when you contact us through Facebook Messenger, Instagram, our website chat, text message or email, and how we use it. Our staff read and answer these messages in one internal inbox.

1. Where your messages come from

  • Facebook and Instagram: messages you send to the ZLM Outdoor Living Facebook Page or the @zlmoutdoorliving Instagram account, and comments on our posts. We receive these through Meta's official APIs, using an app owned by our business.
  • Website chat: conversations with the "Ask ZLM" chat on our website. An AI assistant answers first, and a staff member can take over the conversation.
  • Text messages: texts you send to our customer texting number, delivered through our messaging provider.
  • Email: email you send to office@zlmoutdoorliving.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Who you are: the name, username, profile picture and account ID that Facebook or Instagram shares with us, or the name, phone number or email address you use to contact us.
  • What you send: the content of your messages and comments, including photos or files you attach, and the date and time they were sent.
  • What you tell us about your project: for example your address, the work you're interested in, or a time you'd like to meet.

We do not receive your Facebook or Instagram password, your friends list, or any messages you send to other people.

3. How we use it

  • To read and answer your questions, provide quotes, and schedule appointments and showroom visits.
  • To keep a record of our conversation so any staff member can pick it up.
  • To match your conversation to your customer record in our job-management system, using the phone number or email you gave us, so we can follow up on your project.
  • To understand which channels customers use to reach us, in summary form.

We do not use your messages to send you advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade your information.

4. Who we share it with

Your information is used only by ZLM staff and by service providers that run our systems for us and may use it only for that purpose:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), which delivers the messages you send on its platforms.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure provider (Cloudflare), which stores the inbox.
  • Our email provider (Google Workspace) and our texting provider (Twilio).
  • Our AI provider (Anthropic), which powers the website chat assistant and helps staff draft replies.
  • Our job-management software, where we keep customer and project records.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of ZLM, our customers or others.

5. How long we keep it

We keep conversations for as long as we need them to serve you, to keep records of the work we quote and perform, and to meet our legal and warranty obligations. Messages on Facebook and Instagram also remain in your own account according to Meta's settings.

6. Deleting your information

To ask us to delete the information we hold about you from our inbox and customer records, email office@zlmoutdoorliving.com with the subject "Data deletion request," or call (810) 213-8010. Tell us the name and the Facebook, Instagram, phone or email account you used to contact us. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, except for records we are required by law to keep, such as invoices.

You can also remove our access to your Facebook or Instagram information at any time through your Meta account settings.

7. Security

Only signed-in ZLM staff can open the inbox. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
