How people sign up for deal texts

ZLM Outdoor Living Deals · ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living · November 25, 2026

ZLM Outdoor Living (an outdoor living design, construction and furnishings company with a showroom at 9422 Marine City Hwy, Casco, Michigan) sends deal texts, new-arrival texts and showroom-event texts from (810) 246-5330, up to 4 a month, only to people who sign up. There are four ways to sign up. Each is optional, every checkbox starts unchecked, and none is required to buy anything. We store the date, time, method, wording and IP address or order number of each sign-up, and every opt-out. This page shows exactly what people see. Program terms: Deals by Text Terms · Privacy Policy.

1. Text JOIN to (810) 246-5330

Advertised on our sign-up page and in the showroom. The person gets the welcome message right away; HELP and STOP work on the same number.

JOIN and HELP replies from (810) 246-5330.

2. Sign-up page: booking.zlmoutdoorliving.com/deals

Linked as "Get deals by text" in the footer of every page of our website and online store. The person enters a mobile number and must check the box; the form will not submit without it.

booking.zlmoutdoorliving.com/deals

3. Online store checkout: store.zlmoutdoorliving.com

On the payment step, an optional "Deals by text" box, unchecked. If checked, we use the phone number on that order. The choice is saved on the order.

Store checkout, payment step.

4. Client Hub: clients.zlmoutdoorliving.com

Customers with a Client Hub project page see a separate "Get deals by text?" card with an unchecked box. It is separate from the project-text box, and either can be on or off. They can turn deal texts off from the same page.

Client Hub overview (sample client).

HELP: "ZLM Outdoor Living Deals: For help, call (810) 213-8010 or email office@zlmoutdoorliving.com. Up to 4 msgs/month. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."
