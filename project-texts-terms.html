Project Text Message Terms of Service

ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living · Effective November 16, 2026

These terms cover text messages sent by ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living ("ZLM," "we," "us," or "our"), an outdoor living design, construction and furnishings contractor in Casco, Michigan, to customers who agree to receive texts about their appointments, quotes and projects. Messages may be sent as SMS, MMS, or RCS (together, "Messages"). By opting in, you agree to these terms.

1. Program description

Program name: ZLM Outdoor Living Project Texts. It has two parts:

  • Appointment and Client Hub texts, sent from our customer number: appointment confirmations, reminders and change notices; notices that your Client Hub project page is ready; alerts about your project (schedule changes, new photos or plans, selections or change orders waiting on you, payment reminders, permit and inspection updates); a heads-up when your project team sends you a message in the Client Hub; and, after your project is complete, a request to review our work.
  • Texts with your project team, sent from the work phone lines of ZLM staff (office, sales, foremen and crew): scheduling estimates and visits, on-my-way and arrival notices, questions and answers about your quote or project, and job photos. You can reply, and a ZLM team member will answer.

We do not send marketing or promotional Messages through this program.

2. How you opt in

You opt in in one of these ways:

  • Client Hub: when you open your Client Hub project page (clients.zlmoutdoorliving.com) or the ZLM Client Hub app, you can check an optional, unchecked box that reads: "Optional: Text me about my project from ZLM Outdoor Living at the mobile number above — appointment and scheduling texts, on-my-way notices, project updates, Client Hub alerts and messages from my project team. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Consent is not required to buy anything." You can use the Client Hub without checking the box; we will then reach you by email and in the hub.
  • Quote request: when you request a quote through the chat on our website, you can check an optional, unchecked box with the same wording.
  • Online booking: when you book an appointment at booking.zlmoutdoorliving.com, you can check an optional, unchecked box to receive appointment texts only (see our Appointment Text Message Terms).
  • Signed quote: our quote terms include a Communication section: "By approving this quote, the Client agrees that ZLM Outdoor Living and its staff may contact the Client about this project and related service, warranty and billing matters by phone call, email and text message (SMS/MMS) at the phone numbers and email addresses the Client provided, including texts from the work phone lines of ZLM staff. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts from that number, or HELP for help. This consent is not required to purchase. We do not use these texts for marketing."
  • You text us first: if you text one of our numbers, we will reply to your conversation.

We record the date, time and method of your consent.

3. Message frequency and cost

  • Message frequency varies with your appointments and the stage of your project. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan.

4. Opting out (STOP) and help (HELP)

  • You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any Message. You will receive one Message confirming that you have been unsubscribed from that number, and no further Messages from it unless you opt in again. You can also turn texts off in your Client Hub settings.
  • To opt back in, reply START.
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us at (810) 213-8010 or office@zlmoutdoorliving.com.

5. Supported carriers and devices

Messages are sent as RCS where your device and carrier support it, and as SMS or MMS otherwise. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. Privacy

Your information is handled under our Project Text Message Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
