Project Text Message Privacy Policy

ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living · Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains how ZLM Services LLC, doing business as ZLM Outdoor Living ("ZLM," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you agree to receive text messages (SMS, MMS, or RCS, together "Messages") about your appointments, quotes and projects, as described in our Project Text Message Terms.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information: your name, email address, mobile phone number and property address.
  • Project information: your quotes, appointments, job schedule, selections, change orders, invoices, photos and documents.
  • Consent records: the date, time, and method of your opt-in and of any opt-out.
  • Message information: the Messages we send and any replies, plus delivery status from our messaging provider.

2. How we use your information

  • To schedule appointments and site visits and to send the project Messages described in our terms.
  • To answer your questions and keep your project team informed, including in our job-management system.
  • To keep records of consent and opt-outs and to honor your preferences.

We do not send marketing or promotional Messages through this program.

3. How we share information

  • We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver Messages, such as our messaging platform provider.

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business (such as Twilio and wireless carriers for Messages, Google for email and calendars, Cloudflare and Netlify for hosting, and our job-management software), who may use it only to provide those services, and when required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any Message to stop Messages from that number; reply START to opt back in. You can also turn texts off in your Client Hub settings.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.
  • Opting out of texts does not affect your project; we will keep you updated by email and in the Client Hub.

5. Retention and security

We keep project and consent records only as long as needed for the purposes above, our warranty obligations and the law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
